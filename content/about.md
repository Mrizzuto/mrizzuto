Max Rizzuto is an investigations engineer based in New York. His work centers on online influence, extremism and AI-enabled deception, and he builds the tooling to measure them.

He is currently a contractor building OPSEC and surveillance countermeasures for at-risk communities.

Before that he worked at Brightlines and spent five and a half years at the Atlantic Council’s Digital Forensic Research Lab, where he ran the Foreign Interference Attribution Tracker. He is an AI and disinformation expert with the State Department’s U.S. Speakers Program.
